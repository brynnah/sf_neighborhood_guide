Downtown / Union Square

The shopping and hotel district around Union Square.

About

Downtown here is the retail and hotel core around Union Square, just north of Market Street. Department stores, hotels, and theaters cluster around the square. Cable cars pass on Powell Street, and the blocks step up toward Nob Hill.
