West Portal

General Overview

West Portal grew from the western mouth of the Twin Peaks Tunnel, which opened in 1918 and connected the city’s undeveloped western land to downtown by streetcar. Fernando Nelson and Sons bought about 49 acres and built homes on what had been dunes and vegetable farms. By the 1930s, West Portal Avenue was a walkable commercial strip, with quiet blocks of family homes around it. It still feels like a small town center rather than a long commercial street.

Source: Binning Steam

Food History

By the mid-1900s, West Portal Avenue was a classic neighborhood commercial strip. Early restaurants included French restaurants, Mexican spots such as El Toreador, which has been operating since at least the 1960s, and American-style bakeries and local coffee shops. Later in the 20th century, some of those independent cafes and bakeries gave way to regional chains.

Source: Western Neighborhoods Project

Important Locations

  • Twin Peaks Tunnel, opened in 1918. West Portal station is where Muni Metro comes out of the tunnel.
  • West Portal Avenue, the short shopping street of restaurants, shops, and services.
  • The Empire Theater, opened in 1925.
  • Submarine Center on Ulloa Street, a family-owned shop operating since 1982.
