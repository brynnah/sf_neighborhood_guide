West Portal

A small neighborhood center at the west end of the Twin Peaks Tunnel.

About

West Portal Avenue is a short shopping street of restaurants, shops, and services where the Muni Metro tunnel comes out. The surrounding blocks are houses in the planned neighborhoods west of Twin Peaks. It feels like a small town center rather than a long commercial strip.
