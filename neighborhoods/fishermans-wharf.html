Fisherman's Wharf

The tourist waterfront at the northeast edge of the city.

About

Fisherman's Wharf is the working and visitor waterfront along Jefferson Street, with the fishing harbor, piers, and museums. It faces Alcatraz and the bay, and it connects inland to North Beach and Russian Hill.

Known for

  • The piers
  • Fishing harbor
  • Views of Alcatraz

Places

  • Hyde Street Pier
  • Pier 39
  • Jefferson Street
