Portola

A southeastern residential neighborhood below McLaren Park.

About

Portola is a hillside neighborhood of houses between Bernal Heights, Visitacion Valley, and the Bayview. San Bruno Avenue is the local commercial street. McLaren Park rises along the southern and western edge.

Known for

  • San Bruno Avenue
  • Residential hillsides
  • Edge of McLaren Park
