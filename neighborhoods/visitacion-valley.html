Visitacion Valley

A southeastern residential valley near McLaren Park.

About

Visitacion Valley sits below McLaren Park, near the southern city line. Leland Avenue is the small commercial street. Much of the housing is single-family, with the park hills forming the northern edge.

Known for

  • Leland Avenue
  • McLaren Park edge
  • Southeastern housing

Places

  • Leland Avenue
  • McLaren Park
  • Visitacion Valley Greenway
