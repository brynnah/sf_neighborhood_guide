Western Addition

A central district that includes the Fillmore and Alamo Square.

About

The Western Addition lies west of downtown and Van Ness. It has a deep African American history centered on the Fillmore, and it includes Alamo Square and the Painted Ladies. Redevelopment in the mid-20th century demolished large parts of the neighborhood. Victorian houses stand alongside later housing.
