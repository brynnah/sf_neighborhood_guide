Richmond

The residential avenues between the Presidio and Golden Gate Park.

About

The Richmond runs from the parkside edge of the Presidio out toward the ocean, between the Presidio and Golden Gate Park. Geary Boulevard is the main commercial and transit street, and Clement Street is known for bakeries, restaurants, and groceries. Fog is common, especially farther west.

Known for

  • Clement Street
  • Avenue grid and fog
