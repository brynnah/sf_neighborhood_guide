Richmond

General Overview

The Richmond was first called the Park-Presidio District. It took the name Richmond after an Australian resident began calling his house the Richmond House. The neighborhood was built on the Outside Lands sand dunes and grew quickly after the 1906 earthquake, when people who had lost their homes needed somewhere to live. The Russian Revolution and the Russian Civil War brought immigrants and refugees, and the Russian Orthodox Church Outside Russia later made its headquarters here. After the Chinese Exclusion Act ended, many people of Chinese descent settled in the neighborhood as well.

The Richmond runs from the Presidio out toward the ocean, between the Presidio and Golden Gate Park. Geary Boulevard is the main commercial and transit street. Fog is common, especially farther west.

Source: Richmond District

Food History

Clement Street is the neighborhood’s restaurant, bakery, and grocery row. Mandalay, opened in 1984, was the first Burmese restaurant in San Francisco and is still open. Arsicault Bakery is known for French-style pastries and often sells out early. The Laundromat serves bagels in the morning and pizza at night. Cinderella Bakery and Cafe serves Russian food near Holy Virgin Cathedral. Breadbelly combines coffee and pastries with Asian flavors.

Source: The Infatuation

Important Locations

  • Clement Street and Geary Boulevard.
  • Holy Virgin Cathedral, the Russian Orthodox cathedral associated with the church’s headquarters in the neighborhood.
  • Camera Obscura, a visitor attraction overlooking the ocean at the western edge of the district.
  • Hidden Garden Steps, a mosaic staircase.
  • Mountain Lake Park, at the neighborhood’s edge by the Presidio.
