Sunset

The long residential avenues south of Golden Gate Park.

About

The Sunset runs from Golden Gate Park toward the ocean, a grid of houses on numbered avenues. Irving Street and Judah Street are the commercial rows, and the N Judah streetcar runs through. Fog is common, especially closer to the beach.

Known for

  • Irving Street and Judah Street
  • Numbered avenues
  • Fog and Ocean Beach
