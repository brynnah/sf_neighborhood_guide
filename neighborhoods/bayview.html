Bayview

A southeastern neighborhood along Third Street.

About

Bayview stretches along the southeastern side of the city. Third Street is the commercial spine, with light rail, neighborhood shops, and housing. The area has a long history as a center of San Francisco's African American community, especially after wartime shipyard work.

Known for

  • Neighborhood commercial corridor
  • Southeastern waterfront
