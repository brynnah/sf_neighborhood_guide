Mission Bay / Dogpatch

The old industrial waterfront south of the ballpark, now mixed with new housing.

About

Mission Bay is the newer district of housing, offices, and the UCSF campus built on former rail yards. Dogpatch, just south of it along the Central Waterfront, is an older neighborhood of houses and warehouses turned into workshops, restaurants, and small manufacturers. The two sit side by side on the bay.
