Potrero Hill

A sunny hill south of SoMa, with a small commercial strip on 18th Street.

About

Potrero Hill rises south of South of Market. 18th Street is the neighborhood center for a few blocks of shops and restaurants. The hill is known for weather that is often clearer than the west side, and for views toward downtown.

Known for

  • 18th Street
  • Hilltop views
  • Sunnier microclimate

Places

  • 18th Street
  • Potrero Hill Recreation Center
