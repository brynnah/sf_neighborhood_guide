Japantown

A compact Japanese commercial and cultural district inside the Western Addition.

About

Japantown is centered on Japan Center and the blocks around Post and Buchanan streets. It is one of the few remaining Japan towns in the United States, with shops, restaurants, the Peace Plaza, and community institutions.
