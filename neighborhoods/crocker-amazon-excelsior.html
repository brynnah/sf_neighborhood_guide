Crocker Amazon / Excelsior

Southern residential neighborhoods along Mission Street and the city playground.

About

The Excelsior is a working residential neighborhood with Mission Street as its commercial strip. Crocker Amazon sits beside it, organized around Crocker Amazon Playground near the Daly City border. Both are mostly houses and family streets rather than visitor districts.

Known for

  • Mission Street shops
  • Crocker Amazon Playground
  • Southern residential blocks

Places

  • Crocker Amazon Playground
  • Mission Street
  • Excelsior Library
