Chinatown

One of the oldest Chinatowns in North America, just north of downtown.

About

Chinatown is a dense residential and commercial district between Nob Hill, North Beach, and the Financial District. Grant Avenue draws visitors, while Stockton Street is where many residents shop.

  • Stockton Street markets
