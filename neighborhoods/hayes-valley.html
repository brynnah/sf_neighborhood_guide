Hayes Valley

A small shopping neighborhood just west of Civic Center.

About

Hayes Valley is a compact district of shops, restaurants, and apartments along Hayes Street. The Central Freeway used to cut through it; after that structure came down, the neighborhood filled in with new housing and retail. It sits between the Civic Center and Alamo Square.

Known for

  • Restaurants
