North Beach

The historic Italian and literary neighborhood beside Telegraph Hill.

About

North Beach is wedged between Telegraph Hill, Russian Hill, and Chinatown. Columbus Avenue and Washington Square are the center, with Italian cafes, bakeries, and restaurants. It was also a home for Beat writers in the 1950s. Coit Tower stands on Telegraph Hill at the neighborhood's waterfront edge.
