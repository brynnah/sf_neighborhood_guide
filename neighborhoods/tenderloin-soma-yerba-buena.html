Tenderloin / SOMA / Yerba Buena

The dense districts south and west of Union Square, from the Tenderloin through SoMa.

About

The Tenderloin is a dense residential neighborhood of apartment buildings, restaurants, and social services just west of downtown. South of Market, or SoMa, is the wide grid of warehouses, offices, housing, and cultural institutions south of Market Street. Yerba Buena is the garden, museum, and convention cluster inside SoMa.

Known for

  • Yerba Buena Gardens
  • SoMa warehouses and housing
  • Tenderloin apartments

Places
