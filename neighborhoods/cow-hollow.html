Cow Hollow

A residential pocket between the Marina and Pacific Heights.

About

Cow Hollow sits just south of the Marina, along Union Street. It is mostly houses and low apartment buildings, with a shopping row of boutiques and restaurants. The name comes from the dairy farms that were here before the neighborhood was built.
