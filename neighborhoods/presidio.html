Presidio

A former Army post that is now a national park site at the Golden Gate.

About

The Presidio was a military post under Spain, Mexico, and the United States before becoming part of the Golden Gate National Recreation Area. It is a large landscape of forests, cliffs, and historic barracks. Crissy Field, the Main Post, and the trails toward the Golden Gate Bridge are the parts most people visit.

Known for

  • Golden Gate Bridge approaches
  • Crissy Field
  • Historic military buildings

Places

  • Crissy Field
  • Presidio Main Post
  • Golden Gate Bridge
