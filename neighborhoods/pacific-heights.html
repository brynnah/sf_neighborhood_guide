Pacific Heights

A high residential ridge with large houses and bay views.

About

Pacific Heights occupies the ridge looking north toward the bay. Fillmore Street is the main shopping street. The rest is houses, apartment buildings, and small parks such as Lafayette and Alta Plaza. Many streets are steep, and the views run toward the Golden Gate.

Known for

  • Fillmore Street
  • Lafayette Park
  • Bay views

Places
