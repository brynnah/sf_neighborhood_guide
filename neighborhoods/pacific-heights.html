Pacific Heights

General Overview

Pacific Heights is a wealthy residential neighborhood on the ridge looking north toward the bay, with views of the Presidio, the Golden Gate Bridge, Alcatraz, and the water. It was developed in the 1870s and filled with Victorian houses. Many of those houses were damaged in the 1906 earthquake and rebuilt, though Victorian architecture is still part of the neighborhood. The streets are steep. Fillmore Street is the main shopping street, and Lafayette Park and Alta Plaza are the small hilltop parks. Like the Marina, it is a predominantly high-income, white neighborhood.

Sources: Pacific Heights, Niche residents profile

Food History

Pacific Heights is a large neighborhood, so the dining ranges from fine restaurants to more affordable places, especially around Fillmore Street. The neighborhood itself was not an agricultural district. It bordered Cow Hollow, which was.

Source: SFGATE

Important Locations

  • Haas-Lilienthal House, a Victorian house museum, described as the oldest Victorian in San Francisco that is also a museum.
  • The Mrs. Doubtfire house and the Princess Diaries house, two Pacific Heights houses that draw visitors because of the films.
  • Fillmore Street, the shopping and restaurant row.
  • Lafayette Park and Alta Plaza Park.
