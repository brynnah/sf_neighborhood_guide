Mission

A flat, sunny district with a long Latino history.

About

The Mission is one of San Francisco's oldest neighborhoods, named for Mission Dolores. Mission Street and 24th Street have long been centers of Latino life, with murals, groceries, and restaurants. Valencia Street later became a restaurant and retail corridor. Dolores Park is the main gathering place, and the weather is often warmer than on the west side.

Known for

  • Murals
  • Dolores Park
