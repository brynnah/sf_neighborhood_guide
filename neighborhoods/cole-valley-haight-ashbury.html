Cole Valley / Haight Ashbury

Victorian streets at the east end of Golden Gate Park, and the valley just south.

About

Haight Ashbury takes its name from Haight and Ashbury streets. Haight Street is a row of shops leading to the park, and the surrounding blocks are Victorian houses. Cole Valley is the smaller residential valley just south, with a short cluster of cafes and shops on Cole Street.
