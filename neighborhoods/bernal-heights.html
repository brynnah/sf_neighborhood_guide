Bernal Heights

A sunny hill neighborhood south of the Mission.

About

Bernal Heights is built around a grassy hilltop park with wide views of the city. Cortland Avenue is the village main street, with small restaurants, groceries, and shops. The slopes are mostly houses.

Known for

  • Bernal Hill views
  • Cortland Avenue
  • Residential streets

Places

  • Bernal Heights Park
  • Cortland Avenue
  • Holly Park
