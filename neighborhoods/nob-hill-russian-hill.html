Nob Hill / Russian Hill

Two steep hills above downtown, with cable cars and bay views.

About

Nob Hill holds Grace Cathedral, Huntington Park, and the grand hotels rebuilt after 1906. Russian Hill is just north of it, known for the crooked block of Lombard Street and the Hyde Street cable car. Both are dense with apartments and houses, and both look out over the bay.
