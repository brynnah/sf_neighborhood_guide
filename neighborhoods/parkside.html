Parkside

A residential district in the southwest, around Stern Grove and Taraval.

About

Parkside lies south of the Sunset and north of the lake. It is mostly houses on the avenue grid, with Taraval Street as a commercial and transit corridor. Stern Grove and Pine Lake are the large green spaces nearby.

Known for

  • Stern Grove
  • Taraval Street
  • Low-rise avenues

Places
