About Our Senior Project

We are Sarina and Brynna, and this is a website for our senior project at The Bay School of San Francisco.

Our project is about exploring the different neighborhoods of San Francisco and trying restaurants that represent the history and culture of each neighborhood. We were initially inspired by a previous immersive class we took called Stories of San Francisco, where we learned more about the significance and cultural history of neighborhoods in the city. Even then, we were only able to visit a few, but we learned so much on those field trips. We also didn’t realize how much we didn’t know about our own city. So we decided to get those stories out through our YouTube channel. To keep things tied to today, we also visit restaurants in these neighborhoods. We want to draw more attention to small businesses, so the series leaves out chain restaurants and focuses on local places, from locals ourselves.
